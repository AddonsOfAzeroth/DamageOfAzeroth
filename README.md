# Damage of Azeroth

A damage meter with fight history and statistics for WoW Forever, by DinX.
Works on its own, and matches Casters of Azeroth, Roads of Azeroth and Scrum of Azeroth when they are loaded.

## What it does

- **Meter**: damage, DPS, healing or HPS for this fight or all fights. Click the title for the next view, the time for this fight / all fights. Hover for *Statistics* and *Reset*.
- **Fight history**: every fight is saved when it ends (solo, party or raid), with your spells and everyone's share.
- **Statistics** (`/doa`): your fights with average and best DPS, the DPS curve of one fight, the trend of your last 30 fights, your spells or the group's share.

## Commands

- `/doa`: statistics
- `/doa meter`: meter on or off
- `/doa unlock` / `/doa lock`: move the frames
- `/doa check`: what this client allows

## Rules it keeps

Display only. The numbers come from Blizzard's own damage meter (`C_DamageMeter`). The combat log is not used. Hidden numbers in combat go straight to the bars and are never calculated with.

# Damage of Azeroth (Nederlands)

Een damage meter met gevechtsgeschiedenis en statistieken voor WoW Forever, door DinX.

- **Meter**: schade, DPS, healing of HPS, voor dit gevecht of alle gevechten.
- **Geschiedenis**: elk gevecht wordt bewaard (solo, groep of raid), met je spells en ieders aandeel.
- **Statistieken** (`/doa`): gemiddelde en beste DPS, de DPS-curve van een gevecht, het verloop van je laatste 30 gevechten.

Alleen weergave. De cijfers komen uit Blizzards eigen damage meter; de combat log wordt niet gebruikt.
